# Roadmap and ideas

The game is feature rich and lightly balanced. What it needs most is play and tuning, not more systems. This page is honest about both.

## Known rough edges

- **Balance is a first guess.** Prices, odds, growth times and wages were set by reasoning, not by long play sessions. The cigarette line in particular makes far more than the window sells; the wholesaler and the driver exist to soak that up.
- **Interiors are plain.** The bank, the gun store and the lab are single rooms. They are reached by a fade, not by walking through a real door, because cutting real openings into the town's solid buildings was not worth the risk.
- **The basement is also a fade**, from a hatch in the hall, for the same reason.
- **Traffic is polite and dumb.** Cars keep their lane, stop for you, and do not turn at junctions. They block your car but pass through you on foot.
- **Pedestrians only walk Main Street.**
- **Robbers, Jo and the guard only know the shop.** Nobody but you leaves the building, apart from a robber's getaway car.
- **A forced door breaks open instantly.** Locks keep honest people out; they do not buy time yet.
- **Status screens in the basement and lab cannot be moved** in F2, unlike every other sign.
- **No controller support, no key rebinding, no localisation.**
- **Saves are local only.** There is no export button; see [Save format](Save-Format) for the console way.

## Good next steps

In rough order of value:

1. **A balance pass** with real numbers from real play: how long to the first $10,000, which product dominates, how often robberies land.
2. **Locks that cost time.** A robber at a locked door should spend several seconds on it, which makes the silent alarm and the door panel a real defence.
3. **Robbers who use the town**: arrive by car, case the shop from across the street.
4. **Turning traffic and crossing pedestrians.**
5. **A proper door into the basement and the interiors**, replacing the fades.
6. **A save export and import button** in the pause menu.
7. **Key rebinding and a controller layout.**
8. **More to do in the branch shop** than collect its takings.
9. **Sound**: everything is synthesised, which is charming and thin. A few real samples would go a long way.

## Ideas that were considered and left out

- More weapons, more robber types, more pack varieties: they add volume, not depth.
- A bigger city before the current one has more to do in it.

## Want to help?

Pick anything above, or bring your own. The [Modding guide](Modding-Guide) shows the patterns. Open an issue first if it is big, so two people do not build the same thing.
