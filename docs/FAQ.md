# FAQ

**Windows says it protected my PC.**
The installer is not code signed. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". The source is all here if you would rather build it yourself.

**Where is my save?**
In the app's local storage inside your user profile (`%APPDATA%\RF Grow Co`). It survives updates and reinstalls. See [Save format](Save-Format).

**Can I play in a browser?**
Yes. `npm run serve`, or host the `game/` folder anywhere that serves static files.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. The game makes no network requests at all. The Discord and website buttons open your browser, nothing else.

**The mouse is stuck or a menu will not close.**
Right click closes the open menu or panel. Esc opens the pause menu. Click the scene to capture the mouse again.

**Nobody comes to my shop.**
Open it at the shop control box. After a police fine, customers stay away for a couple of minutes. Storms and snow thin the street out.

**The basement machines will not switch on.**
You need the tobacco manufacturing licence, bought at the office laptop. During a power cut everything stops unless you own the generator in the yard.

**I cannot find the shop control box, a sign, or the staff roster.**
Press F2, look at it, and press Backspace: it goes back to where it started. The control box starts on the security room's north wall.

**My car is wedged in the yard.**
Reverse with S and swing the wheel. From the bay, pull forward a little and steer toward the gate; it opens by itself.

**It runs slowly.**
Pause menu, Settings, set quality to medium or low. That lowers the resolution and the shadows.

**Is this a real business simulator? Is it legal advice?**
It is a game. Nothing in it is advice about anything.

**Can I use the code?**
Yes, it is MIT licensed. Keep the notice. See the [Modding guide](Modding-Guide).

**How do I report a bug or suggest something?**
[Open an issue](https://github.com/TheCodingDad-TisonK/RF-Grow-Co/issues) or say hello on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Th2pnq36).
